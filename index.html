How we build / 02

Designed to grow without breaking what works.

The corporate website is the entry point, not the home for every product's source code. Each division can evolve on its own while sharing a coherent Hephzibah Behulah identity.

Engineering and financial calculations require validation. Educational content needs clear references. AI and cybersecurity work must respect privacy, authorization and human oversight.

  • Independent product repositoriesSimulator, oscilloscope, ElectCAD and other applications can be maintained and deployed separately.
  • One corporate design languageMidnight navy and gold provide the foundation; each division uses its own accent color.
  • Clear source and status informationResearch, educational material and product releases should be documented without claiming unfinished features are available.
  • Accessible interfacesResponsive layouts, keyboard navigation, readable contrast and reduced-motion support.
